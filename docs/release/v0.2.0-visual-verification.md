# dsh-tui v0.2.0 visual verification

This record covers deterministic layout and terminal-behavior checks for the
local v0.2.0 release candidate. dsh-tui is a community client for DeepSeek
Harness; it is not an official DeepSeek client.

## Automated matrix — PASS

- Widths: 120, 96, 80, 60, 48, and 32 columns.
- Themes: transparent Terminal and DeepSeek Light.
- Logo paths: official PNG on supported terminals and the frozen pixel fallback.
- Motion: full, reduced, and off preserve the same information hierarchy.
- Surfaces: welcome, prompt, assistant text, activity, tool cards, approvals,
  history, session details, interruption states, and the compact footer.
- Every tested row stays inside its terminal-cell width with ANSI and CJK text.
- Absolute workspace paths, session IDs, cost, and detailed safety state are
  kept out of the normal footer and remain available through `/status`.

## Visual hierarchy — PASS

- The primary title is `dsh-tui v0.2.0`.
- `Community client for DeepSeek Harness` states the product relationship
  without presenting the community client as an official DeepSeek product.
- The official whale asset and pixel fallback data are unchanged.
- The welcome card remains normal transcript content and scrolls upward with
  the conversation instead of switching screens or becoming fixed chrome.
- The normal footer prioritizes phase, model, and Quick/Deep mode. Elapsed time
  appears only while active; token totals appear only when wide enough.
- Completed tools retain a green success marker but use a neutral surface;
  warning and error colors are reserved for waiting/approval and failure.

## Real PTY behavior — PASS

- Echo and fake ACP sessions preserve input, resize, history, approval, cancel,
  new-session, DeepSeek Light, no-color, and inline-image behavior.
- The 32-column fallback remains usable and does not overflow.
- The welcome card remains visible after the first exchange, then naturally
  leaves the viewport during a longer conversation.

## Owner aesthetic review — pending

Subjective checks remain for the owner on their preferred terminal and font:
official image sharpness, pixel-whale proportions, DeepSeek Light contrast,
and the balance of the 80×24 and 120×30 welcome layouts.
